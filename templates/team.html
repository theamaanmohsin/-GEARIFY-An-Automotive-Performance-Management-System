Frequently Asked Questions

What is Gearify APMS? +
Gearify APMS is an Automobile Performance Management System designed to help you track, manage, and optimize your vehicle maintenance records digitally.
How do I add a new vehicle? +
Simply navigate to the dashboard, click on "Add Vehicle," and fill in your vehicle details including make, model, and registration information.
Can I export my maintenance records? +
Yes! You can download professional receipts for each service and export your complete maintenance history as PDF documents.
How does the service reminder work? +
Our system automatically calculates your next service date based on mileage and time intervals, sending you timely notifications before services are due.
Is my data secure? +
Absolutely! We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data, and all information is stored securely on our servers with regular backups.
Can I manage multiple vehicles? +
Yes! You can add and manage unlimited vehicles in your account, each with separate maintenance records and service schedules.
